# Example campaign brief

**Business:** Independent specialty coffee roaster

**Audience:** City-based professionals who want convenient, traceable coffee

**Objective:** Increase qualified online-store visits over 30 days

**Positioning hypothesis:** Freshly roasted, origin-transparent coffee for people who care about taste but do not want a complicated buying process.

**Content pillars:** Brewing education, origin stories, customer routines, product proof, founder perspective.

**Experiment:** Compare a short brewing tutorial against a founder-led origin story. Keep offer and CTA consistent; compare saves, qualified profile visits and store sessions over a pre-defined period.

**Caution:** This is an illustrative hypothesis, not market research or a performance promise.
